From fatherto son.

Thomas grows up among the chairs of La Bottega dei Parrucchieri, his father Fabio's salon in Pescara. Then he leaves, studies, comes back and opens his own studio.

  1. Pescara

    Starting out in the family salon as a teenager.

  2. Rotterdam

    At 18 he studies the classic old-school style of Schorem in the Netherlands.

  3. Andrea Buluggiu

    He learns the British-style cut from one of its best-known names.

  4. Milan Fashion Week

    Backstage at the shows, with La Bottega dei Parrucchieri.

  5. Via Gobetti 184

    On 8 November 2025 Darta Barber Studio opens, a two-floor studio.
